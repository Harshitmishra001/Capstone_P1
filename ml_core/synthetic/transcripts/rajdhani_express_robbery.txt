### Witness 1
I was just, you know, trying to sleep in my berth, AC first class, coupe B. The train was running very fast. Suddenly, around 11:30 PM, maybe 11:45 PM, there was this massive jerk. The chain was pulled. I mean, who pulls the chain on the Rajdhani at this hour? I looked out of the curtain and saw, um, three guys running down the corridor. They had mufflers over their faces. One of them definitely had a gun, a big one, like a rifle. He was shouting something in Hindi, I think "get down, keep your heads down". I was so scared I just hid under my blanket. I think they went towards the pantry car. They were wearing dark clothes, mostly black. Oh, wait, the last guy had a red jacket on. Yes, a red jacket. I am hundred percent sure. They didn't take anything from our coupe because the door was locked from inside, thank god.

### Witness 2
Sir, my name is Sharma, TC... I mean TTE for coaches A1 to A3. I was checking the charts near the vestibule when the train stopped. It was exactly 11:15 PM, I had just checked my watch because we were running on time. When the train halted, I thought maybe some signal issue. Then I heard commotion from A2. I went there and saw four men. Not three, four. They had country-made pistols, small ones, not rifles. They were wearing jeans and shirts, very normal looking, no mufflers, just some cloth tied around the lower half of their faces. They snatched a gold chain from a lady in seat 14. I tried to intervene but one of them pushed me hard against the door. He said to keep quiet or he will shoot. They were all wearing black jackets. No red jacket at all. They ran towards the AC two-tier coaches after that.

### Witness 3
Arre sir, I was taking the leftover food trays back to the pantry car. It was late, maybe almost midnight. The train stopped so suddenly that the trays fell from my hand. Suddenly two men, just two men, came rushing from the AC compartment side. They had these big knives, very shiny. Not guns, sir, knives. They didn't even look at me. They were running very fast. One was wearing a white shirt and the other had a grey hoodie. They went straight to the back door of the pantry car, opened it and jumped out into the dark. I think the train was stopped somewhere near a bridge, I couldn't see properly outside. They didn't steal anything from the pantry. But they looked very young, maybe college students.

### Witness 4
Okay, so, um, I was on the upper berth, seat 32 in the AC compartment. I couldn't sleep so I was watching a movie on my phone. The train jerked and stopped. It was exactly 11:50 PM. I looked down and saw five men in the aisle. Yes, five of them. They had their faces completely uncovered. I can identify them if you show me photos. They were armed with iron rods. They were shouting at the passengers in the lower berths to hand over their bags. They snatched a laptop bag from the guy sitting below me. The robbers were wearing tracksuits, like they were going for a run or something. One of them yelled "jaldi kar, jaldi kar" and then they just rushed towards the exit. The TTE was nowhere to be seen. It took them barely two minutes.

### Witness 5
I was on duty at the small station we just passed. The Rajdhani doesn't stop here. But I got a call from the control room that the train had stopped midway due to chain pulling. It was around 11:40 PM. The guard called and said some miscreants had stopped the train. When my team reached the spot with the railway police, it was pitch dark. We found some footprints near the tracks. It looked like a group of maybe three people had run towards the nearby village. The passengers were all saying different things. Some said guns, some said knives. But we didn't find any weapons.

### Witness 6
We were all fast asleep. It was my family, my wife and two kids. Suddenly there was a loud bang, like a gunshot, I swear! The train screeched to a halt. It was past midnight, around 12:15 AM. Three guys burst into our compartment. They were huge, very tall, wearing all black, like ninjas or something. They had these scary masks on, like monkey caps. They definitely fired a shot in the air to scare us. My wife was crying. They took her purse and my son's smartwatch. They were carrying big bags, like duffel bags, already full of stolen stuff, I bet. They ran off before anyone could even press the alarm button.

### Witness 7
I am the TTE for the B coaches. I was actually in the washroom when the train stopped. I came out and saw passengers screaming. This was around 11:20 PM. I asked a passenger and he said some dacoits came. I went towards B3 and saw two guys jumping off the train. I couldn't see their faces, but they were short and thin. They didn't have any weapons that I could see. They just grabbed whatever was kept near the doors and ran. It was a very quick operation. I immediately informed the guard on my walkie-talkie.

### Witness 8
Look, I am telling you the truth, I saw everything very clearly. I was awake, reading a book. It was exactly 11:35 PM. Four men walked into our coach. They were very polite, strangely enough. They said "sorry to disturb you, please hand over your cash". They had one small revolver which they showed us just to make a point. They were wearing formal shirts and trousers. I remember one had a striped blue shirt. They took my wallet and just walked away, no running, no shouting. They pulled the chain right after crossing our coupe. I am absolutely certain there were four of them, and no masks.

### Witness 9
I was travelling to Delhi for an interview. My side lower berth was very uncomfortable. Around midnight, the train stopped. I heard people shouting "Chor! Chor!". I peeked out and saw a single guy running. Just one guy! He was carrying a ladies handbag and running like his life depended on it. He didn't have any weapons, nothing. He was just an opportunistic thief, I think. He wore a yellow t-shirt. He jumped out of the moving train as it was slowing down. The chain was pulled later by someone else, I think.

### Witness 10
I was sleeping in the pantry car. The sudden stop woke me up. It was maybe 11:45 PM. I saw three men running through the pantry. They were carrying long swords. Yes, swords! Like the ones in old movies. They were dressed in dirty clothes, like beggars. They were screaming in a language I couldn't understand, definitely not Hindi. They just ran right through, didn't stop to take anything from us. They were heading towards the general coaches.

### Witness 11
It was 11:10 PM. I know because my alarm was set for 11. The train stopped with a huge jolt. A guy, just one guy, came to my berth and put a knife to my throat. He was wearing a red jacket. He told me to give him my gold ring. He smelled strongly of alcohol. He was wearing a black face mask, the covid type. After taking my ring, he just casually walked away towards the washroom area. I think he locked himself in there.

### Witness 12
I was in the guard's van at the rear. I saw the pressure drop in the brake pipe, indicating chain pulling. It was 11:32 PM exactly. I applied the emergency brakes. I looked out with my torch but couldn't see anyone running. Some passengers later came to me saying they were robbed by six men with guns. But I didn't see anyone get off the train. Maybe they hid inside the train itself.

### Witness 13
My wife and I were awake talking in our AC compartment. It was around 11:55 PM. Two women came into our compartment. Yes, women! They were dressed in sarees, their faces covered with the pallu. They suddenly pulled out small knives and asked for our jewelry. It was so unexpected! We gave them a couple of bangles. They quickly moved to the next coupe. Nobody suspects women, right? I'm telling you, it was two women.

### Witness 14
I was in the corridor smoking a cigarette, I know it's not allowed, but still. It was exactly 11:40 PM. The train stopped. I saw five men, big bulky guys, coming from the AC 2 tier side. They had shotguns. Real, actual shotguns. They were wearing military fatigue pants and black t-shirts. They grabbed me by the collar and threw me inside my coupe. They didn't take anything from me, but I heard them break the glass of the emergency window further down.

### Witness 15
I was sleeping. Heard a noise, woke up. It was like 12:30 AM. Saw three kids, maybe 15-16 years old. They had these small wooden sticks. They were just grabbing whatever bags were kept on the floor and running. They looked very scared themselves. One of them tripped over my shoes. They weren't professional robbers at all. Just some local kids who managed to get on the train.

### Witness 16
I was checking tickets in the sleeper class. The train suddenly stopped at 11:25 PM. I went towards the AC coaches because that's where the alarm chain was pulled. I saw four men running away from the train into the fields. They were wearing white kurtas. They looked like politicians or something! They had a big sack with them. No weapons visible.

### Witness 17
I couldn't sleep because the AC was too cold. It was 11:15 PM. Three guys entered. They had these big machetes. They were wearing completely white clothes, like painters. They didn't say a word, just pointed the machetes at us and gestured to hand over the phones. We were terrified. They took my iPhone and left. They were very quiet.

### Witness 18
I was awake, boiling water. Around 11:50 PM, the train stopped. A man ran into the pantry car. Just one man. He had a gun, a small silver one. He demanded all the cash we had in the register. We gave him whatever we had, maybe 5000 rupees. He was wearing a green shirt and jeans. He locked us inside the pantry car and left.

### Witness 19
It was exactly midnight. Four men came in. They had iron rods and started smashing the windows. They were wearing shorts and t-shirts. One had a blue t-shirt with some english words on it. They didn't ask for anything, they just started snatching chains from sleeping women's necks. It was chaos. They pulled the chain and jumped out.

### Witness 20
I was awake. It was 11:45 PM. Two men, very well dressed in suits! They came into the coupe. They had a briefcase. They opened it and took out small pistols. They politely asked for our wallets and watches. They took them, put them in the briefcase, and walked away. No running, no shouting. Just very calm.

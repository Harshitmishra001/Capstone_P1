### Witness 1
Um, yeah, so I was just, like, taking the trash out around—what was it—maybe 11:15 or so? And, uh, it's usually super quiet back there, but I heard this really loud shouting. It was two guys, I think. One was wearing, um, this bright red hoodie, like, almost neon, and the other had a dark jacket, maybe black or navy. They were just going at it, yelling about some kind of debt, I couldn't really hear the exact words because of the [inaudible] from the street. But then, it was like, bang! Just one loud pop. I dropped the garbage bag, it spilled everywhere, and I just froze. The guy in the dark jacket, he just, like, jogged out of the alley. Not even running, just jogging. And then I heard tires squeal, and I swear I saw a silver sedan, like a Honda or something, just fly past the end of the alley. It all happened so fast, you know? Like, one second they're yelling, the next second the guy is on the ground. I didn't go check on him, I just ran back inside and called 911. My hands are still shaking, it's just crazy.

### Witness 2
Okay, so I live on the third floor right above the alley, and I was, uh, I was watching TV when I heard, well, I thought it was firecrackers. You know, kids play with them all the time. It was three pops. Pop, pop, pop. In quick succession. I got up, looked out the window, and I saw, well, I saw three people running away. They weren't fighting or anything before, just standing around in a circle. Two of them ran towards Elm Street and the other one went the opposite way. I think one of them was wearing a white t-shirt, like, a really baggy white t-shirt. The one on the ground was just laying there. Oh, and right after the shots, this dark blue SUV, it might have been a Ford Explorer, it didn't peel out or anything, it just turned its headlights on and drove off kind of slow. I'm positive there were three shooters, or at least three guys running. The SUV didn't pick any of them up, it just left. It's so dark down there, but the streetlight caught the blue paint of the car. I remember that specifically.

### Witness 3

Bam, bam. Buster started barking like crazy. I pulled him back and peeked around the brick wall of the bakery. I saw this really tall guy, like maybe six foot four, he had a, um, a green bomber jacket on. Like an olive drab color. He sprinted out of the alley, almost knocked over a trash can, and he was clutching something to his chest. And there were no cars, no screeching tires, nothing like that. Just the sound of his boots on the pavement. I waited a minute before I looked down the alley and saw the... the body. It was awful. Just awful. Buster wouldn't stop whining.

### Witness 4

So I parked near the alley to check my phone for the building number. And suddenly, there's this flash of light from the alley. Just one flash. I look up, and there's this short dude, maybe five foot five, wearing a black hoodie with the hood pulled way up tight. He comes running out of the alley, nearly trips over the curb, and jumps into the passenger side of this red sports car. I think it was a Mustang, maybe a Camaro? It had those racing stripes down the middle. White stripes. The driver floored it, almost clipped my bumper. I didn't hear any arguments or yelling before the shot, just the single gunshot and then the car speeding off. The short guy had his hands in his pockets the whole time he was running, which looked super awkward. I didn't stick around, I just pulled away and called the cops from the next block.

### Witness 5

He didn't run. He walked back out towards the main street. I didn't see a car or anything, he just blended right into the people walking on the sidewalk. It was cold-blooded, man. Just boom, and he walks off. I dropped my cigarette and just hid behind the dumpster until I heard the sirens.

### Witness 6

### Witness 7
I was driving home from my late shift at the hospital, turning onto 4th Avenue. I saw this black Honda Civic—definitely a Civic, because my sister has the exact same model—parked halfway into the crosswalk near the alley. Two guys jumped out of it. They were both wearing dark clothes, maybe tracksuits? They looked in a huge hurry. They ran into the alley, and like, five seconds later, I hear a single gunshot. Just one. Then they both come sprinting back out, hop into the Civic, and take off running a red light. I didn't see the person who got shot, I just saw those two guys. They didn't seem to have masks on, but I didn't get a good look at their faces. It was too fast. The whole thing from them getting out of the car to driving away was maybe thirty seconds. I remember checking my clock on the dashboard, it was exactly 11:45 PM. I called it in right away.

### Witness 8
So, like, I was walking home from my friend's house. I know it was past my curfew, my mom's gonna kill me, but whatever. I was walking past the alley and I hear this horrible scream. Like, a really loud, desperate scream. I stopped in my tracks, and then I heard a massive bang. It sounded like a shotgun, honestly. It echoed so much. I hid behind a parked car, and this guy comes bursting out of the alley. He was wearing a full-on ski mask, like a black one with just holes for the eyes, and this crazy bright red leather jacket. Like something from a music video. He was holding a huge gun, I couldn't tell what kind. He looked around wildly, then just ran down the street on foot. Didn't get in a car, just ran. I stayed behind the car for what felt like an hour, just shaking. I'm pretty sure I heard a second bang a few minutes later, but it might have just been my imagination or a car backfiring.

### Witness 9
I own the convenience store on the corner. I was locking up the front gate, pulling down the metal grate. I saw a group of people, looked like four guys, arguing aggressively right at the entrance of the alley. They were pushing and shoving each other. Lots of cussing, very loud. I was about to yell at them to take it somewhere else, but then one of them pulled out a weapon. I heard two distinct pops. Small caliber, didn't sound like a cannon. The group scattered immediately. Two went left, one went right. I saw a white van, like a cargo van with no windows in the back, pull up. The guy who shot, I think he was wearing a blue denim jacket, he jumped into the back of the van and it sped off. It all happened in a blur. The poor guy left behind was just slumped against the brick wall. The streetlights flickered, so it was hard to get a clear visual, but I'm certain there were four of them initially.

### Witness 10
I had just parked my car across the street, trying to get to the ATM before midnight. I locked my doors, turned around, and saw this guy walking really fast, almost speed-walking, out of the alleyway. He had a very pronounced limp, like his right leg was stiff or something. He was wearing all black—black jeans, black hoodie, hood up. I didn't hear a gunshot at all. Maybe it was silenced? Or maybe it happened before I pulled up. But he looked super suspicious, kept looking over his shoulder. He walked right past me, didn't make eye contact, and just kept going down the block until he turned the corner at the diner. It was maybe 11:10 PM? Then a few minutes later, I saw police cars swarming the alley. I didn't even realize someone had been killed until the officer asked me to step back. But yeah, the guy with the limp, definitely looked like he was escaping the scene.

### Witness 11
I was right in the middle of a raid in my game, wearing my headset, so it was loud. But I heard these muffled thuds that vibrated through the floor. I live on the second floor, side facing the alley. I thought someone dropped a couch or something. I took my headset off, and I heard someone groan. I opened my window and looked down. It was super dark, but I saw this massive black pickup truck, like an F-150, backing out of the alley. It didn't have its lights on. The engine was rumbling real deep. I couldn't see anyone running, just the truck. I think whoever did it must have been in the truck and shot from the window, or they just hopped right in. The truck hit a garbage can on the way out, then peeled off down the street. I didn't see the victim until the cops showed up with their flashlights.

### Witness 12

We were right by the intersection, maybe fifty feet from the alley. Suddenly, over our yelling, we hear three huge gunshots. Bam, bam, bam. I screamed and grabbed my boyfriend. We ducked behind a mail relay box. I peeked out and saw a silver coupe, maybe a Hyundai, speed out of the alley in reverse, do a crazy J-turn, and tear off down the wrong side of the street. I didn't see any people running, just that silver car driving like a maniac. My boyfriend says he saw a guy in a yellow shirt dive out of the way of the car, but I didn't see that. I just saw the car and heard the three shots. It was terrifying.

### Witness 13

### Witness 14

### Witness 15
I suffer from terrible insomnia, so I often sit by my window with a cup of tea. My window looks directly down onto the entrance of that horrid alley. It was exactly 11:22 PM. I check the clock regularly. I saw a woman, quite slender, wearing a tan trench coat, running frantically out of the alley. She was holding a large handbag against her chest. I didn't hear a gunshot, but my hearing isn't what it used to be. However, her face was illuminated by the streetlight as she ran, and she looked absolutely terrified. She was crying, I could see the tears gleaming. She ran down the sidewalk and hailed a yellow taxi that just happened to be passing by. She got in, and it drove away. I thought perhaps she had been mugged. It wasn't until the police arrived much later that I realized a murder had occurred. I'm certain it was a woman in a tan coat, though.

### Witness 16
I'm training for a marathon, so I do these late-night runs when the streets are empty. I was jogging past the alley, keeping my pace, when I saw two guys standing deep inside the alley. I slowed down because it looked weird. They were hugging. Like, a full embrace, arms wrapped around each other. I thought, 'okay, none of my business,' and kept jogging. But then I heard a muffled pop, like a champagne cork, but deeper. I turned around and looked back. One of the guys just crumpled to the ground. The other guy, he stepped back, adjusted his jacket—it was a very nice, tailored blue suit—and calmly walked deeper into the alley toward the back exit on the next street over. He didn't run, didn't panic. It was the most chilling thing I've ever seen. The hug was just... I don't know, a distraction? Or personal? I don't know.

### Witness 17
I drive for Uber. I was parked down the street, waiting for a ping. My dashcam was unfortunately full and overwritten, so I don't have footage. But I was looking in my rearview mirror. I saw this kid, couldn't have been older than sixteen or seventeen, come sprinting out of the alley. He was wearing a baggy grey hoodie and sagging jeans. He had one hand firmly holding his waistband, like he was trying to keep a gun from falling out, or his pants from falling down, or both. He looked panicked. ### Witness 18
I work security for the First National bank building half a block down. I was doing my exterior perimeter check. I heard four gunshots. Pop-pop... pop-pop. Very distinct grouping. I immediately unholstered my sidearm and took cover behind a concrete planter. I had a clear line of sight to the alley entrance. A grey SUV, looked like a newer model Toyota RAV4, reversed out of the alley so fast it popped up onto the curb and took out a newspaper stand. Tires smoking, the whole nine yards. The driver was a bald man, heavily tattooed on his neck and face. I got a good look at him under the halogen streetlamp. He shifted into drive and floored it eastbound. I radioed dispatch immediately. I didn't see anyone on foot. Whoever did it was driving that RAV4.

### Witness 19

### Witness 20
I was walking back to my apartment and I dropped my wallet near the storm drain. I was bent over, using my phone flashlight to look for it. Then, boom. Just one massive explosion of sound from the alley right next to me. I saw a huge flash of light illuminate the brick wall. I hit the deck, laid flat on the concrete. I didn't see the shooter, but I heard heavy footsteps running *away* from the street, deeper into the alley towards the back side. But then, weirdly, a silver SUV that was parked on the street suddenly peeled out. I don't know if the shooter ran into the alley or got into the SUV. It all happened at the same time. The flash, the footsteps running backward, the car peeling forward. I was just trying not to get shot. I stayed on the ground until the cops showed up.

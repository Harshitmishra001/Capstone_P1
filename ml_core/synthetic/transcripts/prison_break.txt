I started heading down there, and, you know, it was just chaos. Smoke everywhere. I couldn't breathe. Then I saw, I think it was three guys, yeah, three inmates, but they weren't in orange. They had on, like, stolen guard uniforms—the dark blue ones. They were sprinting towards the loading dock. I yelled at them to stop, but, uh, one of them had a fire extinguisher and just hurled it at me. [inaudible] hit my shoulder. I went down hard. By the time I looked up, they had smashed through the chain-link gate and hopped into this white van. It was definitely a white Ford transit van, waiting right outside. I mean, I train for this stuff, but you never expect it to go down like that.

### Witness 2

### Witness 7
Air-One pilot here. We were up over the facility, orbiting at about a thousand feet. Thermal imaging was a mess because of all the fires they set in the yard. Around 21:15 hours, my TFO spotted a heat signature moving fast away from the northern perimeter wall. We pushed in closer. It was a single suspect. Just one guy. He wasn't in a vehicle. He was running along the drainage ditch that leads to the river. We hit him with the spotlight, and he froze for a second. He was wearing dark clothes, couldn't tell if it was a guard uniform or civilian. We directed ground units to his location, but there was a thick canopy of trees and he ducked under it. We orbited for another twenty minutes but lost him. I'm telling you, it was just one guy acting alone, heading for the water. I didn't see any vehicles leaving the area at high speed during that window. All the traffic was incoming police units. We stayed on station until we hit bingo fuel, but no further visual.

### Witness 8
As the deputy warden, I was in the command center. The timeline is critical here. At exactly 20:22, we lost power to Sector 4. The backup generators failed to kick in immediately, which we are still investigating. During that three-minute blackout, a coordinated breach occurred at the sally port. Our exterior cameras came back online at 20:25, and I personally reviewed the footage. There were six inmates. They had managed to strip four of our tactical officers and were wearing their full riot gear—helmets, shields, everything. They just walked right out the front gate! They blended in with the arriving mutual aid units. It was incredibly brazen. They boarded an unmarked white transport van that belonged to the facility, not an outside vehicle. They drove it out themselves. The gate guard just waved them through, thinking they were a response team heading out for perimeter patrol. We realized the error about ten minutes later when we found the real officers tied up in a closet. Six men, full riot gear, white prison van.

### Witness 11

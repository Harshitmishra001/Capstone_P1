### Witness 2

We were going completely normal speed, and then suddenly, there was this violent jerk. Like, it threw me right out of my seat into the aisle. People were screaming, and the lights went out immediately. Before the crash, I didn't see anyone outside, but right after we stopped sliding, I looked out the cracked window. I saw a silver sedan, maybe a Honda, speeding away down the parallel access road. There was only one person in it, a woman, I think, with blonde hair. Uh, wait, maybe it was a man with a hat. It was dark. But definitely a silver car. The smell of diesel was, like, overpowering, and smoke was just filling the cabin. We had to pry the emergency exit open to get out. 

### Witness 3

### Witness 5

### Witness 6

### Witness 8
I was one of the first EMTs on scene. We got the call at exactly 8:34 PM. When we arrived, it was a mess. Three cars off the rails, the engine on its side. Dust everywhere. While we were setting up triage, I noticed the switch mechanism on the track. It looked... tampered with. Like someone had taken a crowbar to it. It was completely bent out of shape. I mentioned it to a cop, but then we had to deal with the injured. The strange thing is, when we pulled up, a black pickup truck, brand new looking, was driving away down the access road with its lights off. I only saw it because my headlights caught the reflectors. Who drives away from a mass casualty incident with their lights off? Anyway, the switch definitely looked broken by force, not by the train hitting it.

### Witness 9
I didn't see anything, I just heard it. I live three streets over. I was watching TV, and suddenly the whole house shook. I thought it was an earthquake, or maybe a plane crash. It was around 8:45, I'm sure of it because my show was almost over. I ran out to my porch, and the sky was lit up orange. I could smell burning rubber and diesel fuel. But the weird thing was, right after the crash, I heard tires screeching on my street. Like someone doing a burnout. ### Witness 10
I was jogging on the pedestrian bridge right over the tracks. It was super scary, honestly. It was exactly 8:12 PM, my Garmin watch tracks it. I looked down and saw the train coming, and then I saw the switch tracks move on their own. Like, they just flipped, right when the train was too close to stop. There was nobody down there. No kids, no guys in hoodies. The switch just changed. The train hit the switch and violently threw itself to the right, smashing into the retaining wall. It was awful, the sound of the metal scraping against the concrete. I didn't see any cars, red, white, or silver, driving away. It seemed like an electrical malfunction or a hack, you know? Like someone did it remotely. There was absolutely nobody on the ground near the tracks when it happened. I had a clear view of the whole area.

### Witness 11
I was in the second car, facing backwards. We were moving along fine, then suddenly the car in front of us just vanished, like it dropped into a hole. We slammed into it, and I was thrown against the seat back. It was pitch black and smoky. After I scrambled out the window, I was standing by the tracks in a daze. I saw a guy in a bright orange jacket, not yellow, but neon orange. He was carrying a heavy duffel bag and power-walking away from the engine. He didn't look back once. He got into a taxi. Yes, a literal yellow cab. It was waiting for him at the crossing. He tossed the bag in and they drove off. It was around 8:30. I told the police about the taxi, but I don't think they believed me. Who takes a taxi to sabotage a train? But that's what I saw.

### Witness 12

The signals were messed up on purpose. 

### Witness 13

### Witness 14

### Witness 17

### Witness 18

### Witness 19

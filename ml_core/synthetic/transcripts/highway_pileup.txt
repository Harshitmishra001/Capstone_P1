### Witness 1
"I was driving the, um, silver Honda... wait, no, my wife's gray Civic, and I was in the left lane, going about, like, sixty-five when suddenly this red pickup truck—or maybe it was an SUV, I couldn't really tell because the sun was in my eyes—just swerved right in front of me without using a blinker or anything. I slammed on the brakes, but the road was slick from the rain—well, it wasn't raining but it was misty, you know, kind of that gross morning dew? And then *bam*, I hit them, and then almost instantly someone rear-ended me, pushing me into the truck again. It sounded like explosions, honestly. Just crunch, crunch, crunch. I looked up after my airbag deflated, coughing from the smoke, and saw this, uh, guy in a yellow jacket running out of a black sedan, looking completely panicked, waving his hands in the air. He seemed okay, physically, but he was yelling about a dog or something running across the road. [inaudible] just chaos everywhere, people honking, glass all over the pavement. The truck that started it, I swear it was speeding, like doing eighty at least before it cut me off. The driver was a woman, blonde, talking on her phone, I saw her looking down right before the impact. I'm absolutely certain of that. She didn't even try to brake."

### Witness 2

It was white with blue lettering, definitely blue, maybe a refrigerated trailer. The truck blew a tire. I heard the *pop* loud and clear over the traffic noise, like a gunshot echoing off the concrete. The truck started fishtailing immediately, like, swaying back and forth across all three lanes, taking up the whole highway. A blue Mustang tried to go around it on the right shoulder and just totally lost control on the gravel, spinning into the middle lane. Then this massive chain reaction started. Cars were just piling into each other, slamming into the Mustang and the semi. I didn't see any red pickup truck at all, nobody was speeding, it was all because of the semi's tire. And there was this guy in a bright green hoodie who jumped out of the Mustang right after it stopped spinning. He was on his phone immediately, looking completely calm, not panicked at all. Not a woman, definitely a guy, brown hair. He didn't even check on the other cars."

### Witness 6

### Witness 7

### Witness 8

The taxi driver, he got out after he stopped spinning, he was wearing a blue sweater, and he looked at his tires like he couldn't believe they failed him. I am completely sure it was an oil slick. I could smell the oil in the air before the crash even happened, it was overwhelming. It smelled like, you know, the inside of a mechanic's garage."

### Witness 9

### Witness 20

It was just a massive, uncontrollable slide. The road instantly turned into a slip-and-slide. Cars were hydroplaning everywhere, spinning in circles.

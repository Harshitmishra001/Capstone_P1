### Witness 1
Sir, I was just doing my morning shift at the gate of bungalow number 4, you know the one near the roundabout. Uh, it was around maybe 7:15 AM or something. I saw this big black Scorpio, very dark black, no number plate on the front. It just came out of nowhere and screeched to a halt right outside the VIP gate. Like, total movie style, sir. Four men jumped out, they were wearing all black, faces covered with monkey caps. Very well built guys, like bouncers or something. They just grabbed the minister sahib as he was stepping out of his porch. The whole thing took maybe... I don't know, thirty seconds? There was a lot of shouting, some abuses in Hindi, and they just shoved him in the back seat. I am 100 percent sure it was a black Scorpio, sir. No gunshots, nothing. Just in and out. They drove off very fast towards the India Gate side.

### Witness 1
So I was just sitting at my counter you know managing the accounts when suddenly I heard this massive sound like a completely loud bang or maybe a firecracker it's hard to say because Diwali is coming up anyway. I looked out from my shop and there was this huge crowd screaming and running towards the Fatehpuri side. And then I saw two guys, I mean I am almost hundred percent sure they were wearing black leather jackets even in this heat, running out of Sethi Jewellers. One of them had a red helmet on and the other was carrying a big blue duffel bag, it looked very heavy. They were shouting something in [inaudible] maybe Punjabi. They jumped onto a black Pulsar bike, no wait it was definitely a red Apache, yeah a red Apache. And they just sped off down the gully. My bhaiya was standing right there he almost got hit. They must have taken lakhs of rupees of gold, I mean Sethi always keeps a huge stock on display.

### Witness 2
Arre sir what to tell you I was just frying the jalebis my oil was very hot. And then this chaos started, people were running left and right like mad cows. I saw three men, yes three men, running out of the jewelry shop. Not two, three. They were wearing typical formal shirts, you know like office goers, white and light blue. None of them had helmets, they were wearing those monkey caps, black color. They were carrying small black backpacks, not one big bag. And they didn't have a bike, they actually ran towards the main road and got into a waiting white Alto car, or maybe it was a Maruti 800. But the car had tinted glasses. It happened so fast within two minutes they were gone. One of them actually dropped something, looked like a silver chain, but some street kid picked it up and ran away before I could say anything. 

### Witness 3
Oh my god it was absolutely terrifying. We are visiting from London and we were just taking photos of the old architecture and the hanging wires. Suddenly there was this loud crack, it sounded exactly like a gunshot. My husband pulled me down behind a handcart. I peeked over and saw these two huge men, very tall, wearing all camouflage clothing like army surplus stuff. They busted out of the jewelry store and one was holding what looked like a real [inaudible] rifle or maybe a shotgun. They had these big burlap sacks, like potato sacks, filled with stuff. They ran straight past us, knocking over a vegetable vendor. They jumped into a yellow auto-rickshaw that was just idling there. The auto driver looked terrified, I think they forced him to drive. It went towards the Red Fort direction. 

### Witness 4
Sir I was just waiting for a passenger near the corner, taking a beedi break. These two boys, very young, barely twenty years old I am telling you, they came running. They were wearing plain t-shirts and jeans, nothing special. But they had surgical masks on, you know the blue ones from COVID times. They were running like the police was already behind them. They didn't have any bags, they were just stuffing things into their pockets, shiny things, gold bangles mostly. They ran past my auto and crossed the divider. I didn't see any bike or car, they just disappeared into the narrow lanes, the paranthe wali gully side. I am telling you they were locals, they knew the gullies very well. 

### Witness 5
I live in the flat right above the sweet shop opposite Sethi's. I was on my balcony hanging clothes. It was exactly 3:15 PM, I know because the school bus had just passed. I saw a black Scorpio stop right in front of the shop. Four men got out. Four. They were all wearing those Zomato delivery t-shirts, red color. I thought they were delivering something huge. But they went inside and two minutes later they came out dragging the security guard, they hit him on the head with a stick. Then they brought out these silver metal suitcases, two of them. They put them in the Scorpio and drove off very slowly because of the traffic. No one even tried to stop them. 

### Witness 6
I am the guard at the saree shop next door. Sir I was alert, I am always alert. But what could I do? These guys were professionals. Three of them, tall, strong built, wearing black masks. They came on two different scooters, Activas, one white, one grey. They parked right on the footpath. One guy stayed outside with a pistol, he pointed it at me and told me in pure Hindi to look down or he will shoot. So I looked down. I heard breaking glass, loud smashing sounds. Then they came out after like ten minutes, it felt very long. They had these yellow plastic bags, like the ones you get from local markets. They sat on their scooters and went towards Chandni Chowk metro station.

### Witness 7
I was inside the shop next door buying a lehenga for my sister's wedding. We heard screaming from outside. The shop owner locked our door from inside. I looked through the glass window. I saw two men in tracksuits, one green and one blue. They were running out of Sethi Jewellers. They were carrying a woman's handbag, a large brown leather tote bag. It was very strange. They were not running very fast, almost jogging. They went into the alleyway next to the jalebi wala. I didn't see any weapons, just them carrying that brown bag. 

### Witness 8
Sir I sweep this road every day. I was sweeping near the drain. This big heavy man, wearing a white kurta pyjama, he came running out of the shop. Just one man. He was sweating heavily and looking back continuously. He had a small black briefcase. He tripped over a stray dog and fell down, the briefcase opened and some rings fell out. He quickly gathered them, swore very loudly in Haryanvi, and started running again. He hopped onto a cycle rickshaw and told the puller to go fast. Just one man, I swear on my children. 

### Witness 9
I pull rickshaw here for ten years. These two men hired me from the metro station. They said they want to go to Sethi Jewellers. They were carrying big empty bags. They looked like normal businessmen, wearing suits, grey and black suits. When we reached, they told me to wait. They went inside. Five minutes later, there is screaming. They come running out, the bags are full and heavy. They forced me to drive them, one put a knife to my neck. I drove them till the main crossing, then they jumped off and got into a running bus, route number [inaudible]. I was so scared I just drove away. 

### Witness 10
I own the electronics shop opposite. It was a well planned heist. Two men in full burqas, yes black burqas, walked into the shop. I thought they were regular customers. But then I saw they were wearing heavy men's boots underneath. After a while, they came out carrying a heavy looking red bag. They walked very briskly towards a waiting silver Innova car. The car had no number plates, I noticed that specifically. They got in and the car reversed out of the lane, which is very hard to do here. Very professional.

### Witness 11
Chai le lo chai. Sir I was giving chai to the Sethi shop staff just five minutes before. When I was walking back, I saw these four boys on two Royal Enfield bullets. The bullets were loud, dug-dug-dug. They stopped, three went inside, one stayed on the bike. The one outside was wearing a yellow helmet and a denim jacket. They came out with two black duffel bags, jumped on the bikes and sped off. They almost hit a cow on the way out. It was definitely around 1 PM, right at lunchtime. 

### Witness 12

They went into the gold shop and came out with bags of gold. They didn't run, they walked like kings. Nobody stopped them. They went towards the river. 

### Witness 13

One of them had a silver pistol. He fired one shot in the air, bang! Everyone scattered. They grabbed a red Apache bike that was parked there, hotwired it in like two seconds, and zipped away. They were carrying a small grey backpack. 

### Witness 14

They didn't get back in the auto, they just started running down the street, weaving through the crowd. They were wearing plain clothes, maybe brown shirts. ### Witness 15

They ran past me, they smelled like strong perfume and sweat. They got into a cycle rickshaw and disappeared into the crowd. 

### Witness 16
Traffic police on duty. I heard the alarm from Sethi Jewellers. I ran towards the shop but the crowd was running in the opposite direction, blocking me. I saw a white Swift Dzire speeding away from the side lane. I couldn't note the number plate because of the dust. I was told by bystanders that three men in black suits were in that car. 

### Witness 17
I was guiding a group of French tourists. We were right outside. Two men wearing monkey caps and tracksuits ran out. They were carrying nothing, their hands were empty! But their pockets looked bulging. They pushed one of my tourists and ran towards the Jama Masjid side. It was pure chaos. 

### Witness 18

They didn't use a vehicle, they ran through the interconnected rooftops. I saw them climbing the drainpipe of the old haveli. 

### Witness 19

She walked out calmly carrying a massive brown leather bag. She got into a waiting Uber, a white WagonR, and just left. Nobody suspected her until the alarm went off. 

### Witness 20
